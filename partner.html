Three ways to be in it with us.

The Clock Institute is built on company, and that includes the company we keep at the institutional level. We work with funders, district networks, and vendors who meet a fiduciary standard. Each relationship is specific.

01The three lanes
i.

Foundation & co-brand partners

Network introductions, co-branding on program materials, inclusion in educator-facing communications. We share storytelling rights and a year-end impact report. Our inaugural partner is the Classroom of the Future Foundation.

Currently in conversation with 1 partner
ii.

District & SDCOE partners

We position our programs as optional supplemental mentorship — distinct from credential induction. We seek letters of support, briefings with district leadership, and pathways to teacher recruitment that respect existing structures.

Outreach opens summer 2026
iii.

Vendor partners

Eight premium vendor slots per event, with category exclusivity. Financial services partners must meet a fiduciary standard. Commissioned annuity products are explicitly prohibited. Curated categories: credit union, fiduciary advisor, PSLF navigator, classroom supplies, EdTech, graduate education, home lending, experiential.
