Partner

Partners who show up for teachers.

We work with three kinds of partners — foundations, school districts, and the organizations whose products and services genuinely help new teachers. Every partner shares a commitment to teachers and to a fiduciary standard for anything offered to them.

01The three lanes
01

Foundation partners

Foundations help us build and run programs at scale. Partners receive co-branding, named recognition in educator communications, a year-end impact report, and shared rights to outcomes and storytelling. We’re currently in conversation with the Classroom of the Future Foundation as our inaugural partner.

In conversation · 2026
02

District partners

Our programs are optional supplemental mentorship, distinct from formal credential induction. We work with districts and the County Office of Education to make sure that’s clear, and to make it easy for new hires to apply if they’re interested.

Outreach opens summer 2026
03

Program partners

A small, curated group of organizations join each cohort event to meet teachers directly — credit unions, fiduciary financial advisors, student loan and PSLF specialists, classroom supply companies, EdTech tools, graduate education programs, home lenders, and travel programs for teachers. Each category is exclusive, and any organization offering financial products must meet a fiduciary standard. We don’t accept commissioned annuity products.
