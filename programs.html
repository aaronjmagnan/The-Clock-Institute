Program 001 · 2026–27

Teacher of the Future.

Thirty new teachers across San Diego County, paired with six principal mentors for one school year. Two cohort events, a small-group dinner mid-year, and direct phone access to a mentor whenever the day calls for it. A real cohort, not another induction program.

01The shape of it
30
Teachers

First-year teachers from across San Diego County. One shared cohort for the full year.

6
Mentor principals

Experienced principals, hand-picked. Each one mentors a pod of five teachers.

2
Anchor events

A fall kickoff and a spring close, with a small-group dinner between.

1yr.
Cycle

One full school year, fall through spring. A real commitment, time-bounded.

02The arc
Phase 01

Fall 2026

Event One — Launch.

A full-day kickoff. Panel discussion, small-group case studies, conversations with our partner organizations, a session on how the program works, and a matching mixer. Teachers learn their mentor and pod assignment at the end of the day.

Phase 02

Winter 2026–27

Pod Dinner.

Each pod meets at a local restaurant for a facilitated dinner. Everyone shares one challenge they’re working through and one recent success. The mentor collects written reflections to bring back at the spring event.

Phase 03

Between

The year, in between.

Each pod runs its own group chat. Teachers can call or text their mentor anytime. We check in monthly but mostly stay out of the way.

Phase 04

Spring 2027

Event Two — Ascend.

A spring closing event. Teachers share growth reflections developed with their mentor, the cohort celebrates completion, and the program transitions into an alumni network they can return to.

03How we’ll know

We measure relationships, not attendance.

80%

of mentor-teacher pods complete the full year together.

70%

of teachers rate the mentor relationship “meaningful” or stronger at year-end.

80%

of mentor principals sign on to return the following year.

Also tracked · pre/post survey of teacher confidence · 1-, 2-, and 3-year retention · partner satisfaction and renewal
