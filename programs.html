Program 001 · 2026–27

Teacher of the Future.

Thirty newly hired teachers from across San Diego County, matched with six principal mentors for a year of relationship-led development. Two anchor events. A mid-cycle dinner. Open access to a mentor who picks up the phone. Not another induction. A cohort.

02The arc
Phase 01

Fall 2026

Event One — Launch.

Panel keynote, structured case work in mixed small groups, curated vendor rotations, Clock Institute values workshop, mentor matching mixer. Pods of five teachers per mentor revealed at closing.

Phase 02

Winter 2026–27

Pod Dinner.

Each pod gathers at a local restaurant for a facilitated dinner. Every person shares a specific challenge and a recent success. Reflection cards are collected by the mentor and returned at Event Two.

Phase 03

Between

The pod, in motion.

Each pod maintains a private group chat. Teachers have open phone and text access to their mentor. Pods are self-directed, with light-touch Institute support.

Phase 04

Spring 2027

Event Two — Ascend.

Second vendor cycle, teacher growth reflections developed with mentors, completion recognition, and scholarship disbursement. The cohort closes — and a network begins.
