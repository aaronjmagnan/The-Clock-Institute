01What we believe

The first year of teaching determines the next twenty.

Most new teachers are handed compliance-based induction and left to figure out the rest alone. We think the real shape of a teaching career is set in the first year — by who picks up the phone, who shows up to dinner, who tells the truth about what just happened in third period.

The Clock Institute exists to build that company. Deliberately. At scale. Across a career.

02How we measure

We count in years.

Every program the Institute builds is measured against its effect on year twenty, not year one.
