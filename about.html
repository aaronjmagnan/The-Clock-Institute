About

An institute of company, over time.

The Clock Institute is a 501(c)(3) organization that recruits mentors and builds networks among educators to promote equity, longevity, and success in the field. Our governance is independent of any single district — by design.

01What we believe
i.

Plain.

We do not perform certainty we have not earned. No jargon. No “transformative ecosystems.” We name what changed.

ii.

Patient.

Careers are long. We measure ourselves at year twenty, not year one. Programs are designed to compound.

iii.

Specific.

Names. Places. Sums. Six principals. Thirty teachers. One year. We commit publicly to numbers because anything else is decoration.

iv.

Independent.

Governance is separated from any one district to keep the work honest. We answer to teachers and to the long arc.
