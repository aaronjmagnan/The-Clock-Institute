About

An institute for educators.

The Clock Institute is a 501(c)(3) based in San Diego. We mentor educators across their careers and connect them with the schools and leaders who hire them — teachers and administrators alike. Independent of any single district by design. Our first program launches in fall 2026.

01What we do

We build mentorship programs for educators, and run them ourselves.

Our first program, Teacher of the Future, launches in San Diego County in fall 2026. Thirty first-year teachers paired with six experienced instructional leaders for a full school year — two cohort gatherings, a mid-year dinner, and direct access to a mentor between events.

We’ll run it, study it, and use what we learn to build the next one.
